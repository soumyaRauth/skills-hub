SEO Growth Engine

Sustainable search visibility for a website and the person behind it, measured honestly. It runs your SEO as a long-term project: it finds what actually stands between you and search results, does the safe work in your site’s own code, asks before anything risky, and writes everything down so the next session picks up where this one stopped.

Soumya Rauth · Creator & maintainer
npx skills add soumyaRauth/skills-hub --skill seo-growth

The problem

an audit ≠ a fix · a keyword list ≠ a plan · a change ≠ a result

SEO tools report, SEO advice generalizes, and both forget. Meanwhile the problem is usually small, specific and invisible: 

about.html   <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> left over from staging
index.html   <title>Home</title> on the page meant to answer a search for your name
article      canonical points to http://www. while the site serves https://
sitemap      lists the hidden page, omits the only article
search       other people share your name; your site is not on the first page

One of those lines outweighs every keyword in a content plan. And a change made today is judged a month from now, by someone who no longer remembers what the baseline was.

How it works

read the records  →  what changed  →  verify issues  →  rank the next action
   →  do the safe work  →  ask for the rest  →  validate  →  write it down  →  one next step

Every claim carries a label, so you always know what is known:

LabelMeans
VerifiedObserved this session, or in data you supplied, with source and date
EstimatedA third-party tool’s figure, with the tool named
InferredReasoned from evidence it names
HypothesisPlausible, untested; usually becomes an experiment
UnknownNot available, and what would make it known
No number without a source. Search volume, rankings, traffic, backlinks and indexing status come from Search Console, a tool you already have, or an observation with a date. Otherwise they are unknown, and the report says so.

A project, not an audit

Records that remember

.seo-growth/<domain>/: profile, strategy, a scored backlog with a “Now” list of five, an append-only history, dated baselines, content inventory, keyword map, competitors, personal brand, authority opportunities, experiments and reviews. Plain Markdown. One folder per site, never mixed.

Your name, as a target

Name-search snapshots, other people who share the name, identity consistency across GitHub, LinkedIn and the rest, a canonical bio written only from verified facts, brand pages, and Person structured data. It prepares the profile text; you make the changes.

Without askingAsks firstNever

What it will not do

  • Invent metrics. No made-up search volumes, rankings, traffic, backlinks or indexing status
  • Promise rankings. It says what a change should influence and how it will be measured
  • Claim a win it cannot attribute. Experiments have baselines, evaluation windows after recrawl, and a control; one snapshot is not a trend
  • Fabricate you. Bios, pages and articles use your facts; anything only you know becomes a question or a placeholder
  • Act outside your site. No outreach sent, no accounts created, no profiles changed, nothing published or bought without your yes
  • Break the site for SEO. It reads the framework and conventions first, builds, and compares before and after
  • Use spam tactics, even when asked. It names the risk in one line and offers the genuine alternative

Limitations

  • Without Search Console it cannot see what Google sees: indexing, real queries, impressions and clicks. It says so, tracks what it can, and makes connecting it an early task
  • Search results vary by person, place, device and time; its observations are dated snapshots, and its search tool may not be Google
  • No search volumes without a keyword tool; demand is scored from evidence instead
  • Pages rendered by JavaScript can look empty to its fetcher; it checks with a browser or the source before calling content thin
  • It prepares profile text and outreach; you act on third-party sites, or authorize it
